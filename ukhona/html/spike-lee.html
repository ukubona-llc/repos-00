The ICE Resolves on the AP

Four bars in, Future has mentioned spike (ice), then Ice T, then a fully loaded AP — a watch covered in diamonds, meaning covered in ice.

The whole verse is a chain: spiked tea → iced tea → iced out watch. Three instances of "ice" disguised as celebrity names and jeweler's slang. The AP is the punchline to the Spike Lee setup. He threaded the word ice invisibly through four bars without saying it once.

Historical Context · 2014
The Real Drought
In 2014, Actavis — the most coveted brand of promethazine-codeine syrup used to make lean — permanently halted production under pressure from advocacy groups. The supply collapse was immediate and real. Street prices for remaining supply multiplied. The culture went dry.

Future released DS2 in 2015, when the drought was still actively felt. Opening his album with "I thought it was a drought" — while describing a cup full of purple Actavis — was the ultimate power statement: the shortage is something that happened to other people.
Final Assessment

Greatest Opening Verse in Trap

Four bars. Three meanings per line. A phonetic pun disguised as two celebrity name-drops. A recipe for lean told in the names of two famous men. An "ice" motif threaded invisibly through a flex about a watch. And a punchline about a real historical shortage that confirms he is outside the laws of supply and demand.

Gemini found the surface. The compression was deeper. The greatest trap star of all time opened his masterpiece with a compression so dense that it took ten years to fully decode.
