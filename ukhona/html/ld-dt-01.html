§ 02
Two Problems, One Architecture
Pre-Donation · Stages I–III
Landscape Localization
The pre-donation problem is a classification task: is this donor's θ positioned within a safe region of L(θ)? The gradient ∇θL orients the evaluation — it describes the slope, not the trajectory. No descent has yet occurred. Eligibility is not optimization; it is localization.

For older donors (≥55), the K08 grant identifies heightened glomerular senescence as a landscape deformation — the safe region is smaller, the slopes steeper. NHANES priors define the manifold; individual labs locate the donor on it.
Post-Donation · Stages IV–V
Stochastic Optimization Loop
After nephrectomy, the donor has a new initial condition θpost. The ε perturbation has known statistical properties from post-nephrectomy literature — this is not uncertainty, it is structured shock. The remaining kidney hyperfiltrates; the recovery manifold is estimable.

Now the true SGD loop begins. Every lab, every wearable signal, every sentinel hospitalization is a stochastic update. The model learns the individual. The pre-donation twin becomes the counterfactual control.
§ 03
The Kahneman Problem — Not a UI Issue

The 2017 KDIGO guidelines ask a population question: "How many ESRD events in younger donors may be averted by transplanting older donors instead?" The number produced is not merely hard to communicate — it is epistemologically invalid as personal guidance. Not even a professional can correctly interpret a population lifetime risk estimate applied to the individual sitting in front of them. This is not a display problem solvable by better UI. It is a category error.

The individualized loss function L(θ) is not a friendlier wrapper around the same statistic. It is a different quantity entirely — a personal risk surface computed from the donor's own parameter vector, updated continuously, benchmarked against their own counterfactual. The LLM translation layer exists to render gradient-space updates into clinical language, but the underlying transformation from population to individual has already happened mathematically, before any display occurs.

§ 04
The Counterfactual Control — The Methodological Revolution

Every existing dynamic risk model benchmarks against external population controls — matched cohorts, age-adjusted referents, NHANES surrogates. This introduces fundamental confounding: the controls did not donate, did not undergo nephrectomy, did not experience the ε perturbation.

The digital twin architecture implies something more rigorous: the donor's own pre-nephrectomy twin is the control group. Post-donation trajectory is benchmarked against the counterfactual unperturbed trajectory — what this person's eGFR, frailty index, and cardiovascular risk would have been had they not donated. The deviation between the live twin and the counterfactual twin is the true signal. Sentinel events register as divergences from the counterfactual, not as deviations from a population average.

This is not a marginal methodological improvement. It changes the scientific question from "is this donor doing as well as similar people?" to "is this donor doing as well as they would have?" — a fundamentally different and more answerable question.

§ 05
Donor Lifecycle as Optimization Trajectory
Evaluation · θ₀
Baseline Parameter Estimation
NHANES priors + individual labs establish θ₀. For older donors, glomerular senescence is a landscape feature, not a disqualifier — it shapes the curvature of L(θ). The gradient ∇θL locates the candidate relative to the safety boundary.
Eligibility · L(θ₀)
Safe Region Localization
Clearance for donation is confirmation that θ₀ lies within the acceptable region of the loss surface — not that the donor is optimal, but that the perturbation ahead is survivable and recoverable within known bounds.
Nephrectomy · ε
Structural Perturbation — The Hinge
Immediate nephron mass reduction (~50%). eGFR drops to 50–60% of baseline. Compensatory hyperfiltration follows: recovery to ~70–80% within 6–12 months. The post-nephrectomy literature constrains the expected perturbation manifold — ε has known distributional structure. This is the new θ₀.
Recovery · θ₁…θₙ
Counterfactual Divergence Tracking
The live twin updates with each lab, BP reading, medication change, wearable signal. The pre-donation twin continues its counterfactual trajectory. Divergence between the two is the primary outcome variable — replacing external comparators entirely.
Long-Term · Ecosystem
Lifelong Adaptive Model
Life perturbations — hypertension onset, pregnancy, infection, aging — enter as εₜ terms. The model does not stop at 2 years. The KDIGO mandate is the floor, not the ceiling. The twin is the permanent scientific record of the donation's effect on this individual.
§ 06

The LLM API layer does not summarize population statistics. It renders the local gradient — the rate and direction of the donor's divergence from their own counterfactual — into clinical language at a resolution no prior risk calculator has achieved.

§ 09
Grant Context — K08-AG065520
K · K08-AG065520 · 2020
Perioperative and Long-Term Risks in Older Donors
The grant's central move: shifting from the KDIGO population question to the individual question — "Can I safely donate?" — and identifying that ~80% of older donors will cross the GFR < 60 threshold post-nephrectomy, not because they are sick, but because the population threshold was never calibrated for post-donation physiology. The grant named the gap. This architecture fills it.
∇ · Gradient Insight · 2022–2023
Traversal, Not Visualization
The high-dimensional NHANES parameter space is not a visualization problem. It is a traversal problem. The gradient gives direction without requiring full landscape visibility — the foundation of the stochastic approach to sparse clinical data.
ε · Perturbation Formalization
Nephrectomy as Initial Condition
The post-nephrectomy literature provides the distributional structure of ε: immediate 40–50% GFR reduction, hyperfiltration recovery to ~75% by 6–12 months. These are not arbitrary parameters — they constrain the recovery manifold and allow the model to distinguish expected perturbation from anomalous divergence.
