II. The Epsilon Problem

The omitted +ε is the most theologically and mathematically interesting element. Here is why it must be included in "Be" and recovered in "Build":

Why ε Cannot Be Ignored

Differentiation is a lossy operation with respect to constants. When you differentiate f(t) = eᵗ + C, the C vanishes. When you differentiate [xⁱ + ε, f(t | xⁱ)], the ε — if treated as a constant shift — disappears through stages II–IV. This is mathematically correct but existentially catastrophic: a system that strips away its noise during transformation and fails to reintegrate it during construction will be brittle. It will not account for drift, bias, stochastic input, or the irreducible uncertainty of the initial conditions.

Solomon's temple had tolerance margins. Every great build does. The ε is that margin.
